# Smart Euclid — Application Design Photo

## Design Direction
The visual identity for Smart Euclid draws from contemplative, timeless black-and-white photography of young students engaged in focused work.

### Recommended Hero / Marketing Photo
A high-quality black-and-white portrait of a young person (late teens / early 20s) with curly dark hair, wearing a textured sweater, sitting outdoors on a stone ledge and carefully drawing geometric diagrams in a notebook. Soft overcast light, shallow depth of field, classical architecture softly blurred in the background.

This matches the aesthetic of the two reference images provided (student writing near Big Ben + child with flowers in a field) and reinforces the academic, thoughtful, research-oriented spirit of the Grade 12 STEM project.

### How to use in the app
1. Place the final photo as `assets/hero.jpg` (or replace the existing root JPG).
2. Reference it in `index.html` onboarding step 0 or as a subtle background element on the home screen.
3. Keep the futuristic cyan / violet UI overlaid; the photo provides the human, philosophical counterpoint.
